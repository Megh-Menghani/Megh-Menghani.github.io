Isotope (Extraction Shooter) Advanced Inventory System

In an extraction shooter, how fast you can loot decides whether you get out alive. Over three months I took a marketplace inventory plugin (IFP) and made it handle like Escape from Tarkov's, in replicated C++.

  • Windows-style marquee selection that skips empty tiles and works in your own bags and in external containers.
  • Quick transfer, quick loot and auto-stacking, with a queue that sends items to the last container you used.
  • Shift-drag splits a stack in half, the scroll wheel changes the count mid-drag, Ctrl-drag takes one, Alt moves a batch.
  • Packing with rotation and ghost previews when you drag several items at once, plus sorting by name, category, weight, rarity, stack size or price.

Client project, NYC / C++ / Replication / UMG
